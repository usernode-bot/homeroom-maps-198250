# Homeroom Maps icon

A white filled dot centred on a flat indigo tile. This is the same mark the
page uses as its tab icon, drawn at full bleed for the home-screen tile.

- Background: flat indigo `#6366f1`, opaque, edge to edge.
- Glyph: one white circle, centred, radius 92 px on the 512 px tile.
- No gradient, lettering, border or second symbol.

The tab icon in `public/icon.svg` is the same geometry on a 100-unit grid: an
indigo circle of radius 45 and a white dot of radius 18.
